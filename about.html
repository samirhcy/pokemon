History of Pokémon

Pokémon was created in 1996 by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak, with publishing by Nintendo.

The first games, Pokémon Red & Green, were released in Japan and quickly became a global success. Inspired by his childhood hobby of collecting insects, Tajiri created a world where players could capture and train creatures known as Pokémon.

Over time, the franchise expanded into an international phenomenon including trading cards, anime, movies, and video games. Today, Pokémon remains one of the most successful entertainment franchises in history, with over 1000 Pokémon across multiple generations.
